## Purpose of Database Systems

The purpose of this course is to encourage the development of your in-depth understanding of the design and implementation of database management systems.

## Purpose of Database Design and SQL

The purpose of this course is to introduce you to standard notations, e.g., SQL, that implement essential relational algebra operations, as well as their limitations and use in the organization and retrieval of large 
amounts of data.

## Purpose of Database Management Systems

The purpose of this course is to encourage the development of your in-depth understanding of the design and implementation of database management systems.

## Advanced Database Systems

The purpose of this course is to encourage the development of your ability to implement complex techniques of storage and retrieval of data for decision making in a business organization.
